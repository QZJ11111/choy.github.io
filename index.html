Welcome to my homepage! I am a student/researcher in theoretical physics, focusing on the mathematical structures that underpin quantum theory and their manifestations in quantum field theory and quantum phases of matter. My recent interests center on Lie algebras and representation theory, gauge symmetries (e.g. SU(3)), and topological field theories and their lattice realizations. I am also broadly interested in the interplay between physics and machine learning, especially physics-informed learning and neural operators.
